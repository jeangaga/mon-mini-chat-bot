Dominant equity driver.

### C. Financing fears spread across the infrastructure chain

The scout identified weakness in:

CRWV, NBIS, ORCL, IREN, VRT, SMCI, DELL, and AI-exposed electrical/power infrastructure names.

This is not one homogeneous business model.

- CRWV: highly leveraged compute provider.
- ORCL: large cloud expansion commitments.
- VRT/DELL: infrastructure equipment exposure.
- PWR/CAT/GEV: selected beneficiaries of power and construction demand.

The common equity mechanism is concern about the durability and financing of the investment cycle.

OWNER PM READ-THROUGH

The bull thesis is no longer being tested principally by whether AI demand exists.

It is being tested by:

How quickly demand turns into customer cash flows, which companies fund capacity, what financing costs, and who retains the resulting economic profit.

The implications differ enormously across hyperscalers, neoclouds, GPU suppliers and project-finance investors.

### COUNTER-INTUITIVE

1\. Hot PPI → lower long-end yields.

An equity-to-bond flight rather than conventional inflation repricing dominated the Treasury market that day.

2\. Lower yields did not restore semiconductor leadership.

Thursday's aggregate SPX rebound did not translate into sustained semiconductor strength.

3\. Strong semiconductor earnings → lower stock prices.

Demand and order visibility were not the binding variables for the immediate market reaction.

4\. Strong bank earnings produced very different reactions.

GS was rewarded for equities trading, JPM initially moved little despite a trading miss, while WFC declined on a revenue disappointment.

This suggests unusually high selectivity rather than indiscriminate aversion to financials.

5\. Defensive strength coexisted with weakening small caps.

The Dow outperformed, but Russell lost 3%.

The rotation therefore cannot yet be called broad cyclical improvement.

### CURRENT REACTION FUNCTION

The regime has changed from AI earnings overcoming rates pressure to AI financing/return concerns overwhelming near-term earnings strength.

Three testable rules:

AI credit deteriorates → AI equities underperform, even when Treasury yields fall.

Strong AI earnings no longer guarantee a positive stock reaction if financing, margin durability or positioning dominates.

The aggregate SPX can resist selective AI weakness temporarily through defensives and non-tech rotation, but cannot indefinitely absorb liquidation of its largest earnings leaders.

Luis Alvarado / Wells Fargo Investment Institute, in scout-captured secondary reporting, described a shift toward greater scrutiny of each incremental dollar lent.

- Equipment suppliers selling into that capacity.
- Credit investors bearing different collateral, lease and counterparty risks.

Nevertheless, supplier equity valuations can fall if even the marginal buyer's financing deteriorates.

## 9. PM CARRY / NEXT WEEK

### BASE CASE

The market has entered an AI financing/valuation correction amplified by crowded positioning, rather than a demonstrated broad earnings contraction.

The immediate evidence supports keeping a more cautious tactical stance on highly leveraged AI infrastructure and crowded semiconductor exposures.

But it does not support treating all AI-related earnings streams as economically equivalent.

Distinguish:

- Cash-rich hyperscalers and application/platform owners.
- Chip suppliers with current strong earnings but demanding expectations.
- Leveraged capacity developers.
- Infrastructure/power equipment providers.
